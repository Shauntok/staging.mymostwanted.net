# Region flag artwork and credits

The Malaysia state and Federal Territory SVGs are from [jwarby/malaysia-state-flag-icon-css](https://github.com/jwarby/malaysia-state-flag-icon-css), under `flags/1x2/`. Its [Credits section](https://github.com/jwarby/malaysia-state-flag-icon-css#credits) identifies each source image, author, and license.

The upstream project lists the following images as CC BY-SA 3.0: Johor (Mrmw / Mike Rohsopht), Kelantan (Mysid), Sarawak (Matthew A. Lockhart), Selangor (Mysid), and Terengganu (Mysid). The other Malaysian region flags are marked Public Domain by the upstream credits. The SVGs are included unchanged.

Singapore uses `sg.svg` from [lipis/flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), under that repository's MIT license; see [LICENSE-flag-icons.txt](./LICENSE-flag-icons.txt).
